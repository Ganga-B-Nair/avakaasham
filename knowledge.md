1. KERALA SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS (old age, widow, disability, unmarried women 50+, agricultural labourers). Disbursed via Local Self Government (Grama Panchayat / Municipality / Corporation Secretary). Since Aug 2026, paid by Direct Benefit Transfer to Aadhaar-linked bank accounts; bedridden beneficiaries can still get doorstep delivery. Common stoppage reasons: annual mustering (biometric life verification at Akshaya) not done, Aadhaar not linked to bank, income certificate/eligibility re-verification, duplicate entry. Bedridden persons can request home mustering. Persons with learning difficulties should not be denied pension merely because they cannot complete mustering (Kerala State Human Rights Commission, Mar 2026). Office code: PEN.
2. RIGHT TO INFORMATION ACT, 2005. Sec 6: application to Public Information Officer (PIO) of any public office. Sec 7(1): reply within 30 days (48 hours if life or liberty is involved). Sec 19(1): First Appeal to the Appellate Authority within 30 days after the deadline lapses. Kerala application fee Rs 10; BPL applicants exempt. Use to get file status, reasons for delay, names of officers responsible. Office code: RTI.
3. MAINTENANCE AND WELFARE OF PARENTS AND SENIOR CITIZENS ACT, 2007. Sec 4: children/relatives must maintain parents and senior citizens. Sec 5: application to Maintenance Tribunal (in Kerala, Revenue Divisional Officer / RDO); application to be disposed of within 90 days of notice. Sec 9: maintenance up to Rs 10,000 per month. Sec 17: no lawyer needed. Sec 23: if property was transferred on condition of basic care and the transferee fails to provide it, the Tribunal can declare the transfer void. Sec 16: appeal to Appellate Tribunal (District Collector) within 60 days. Office code: MNT.
4. RIGHTS OF PERSONS WITH DISABILITIES ACT, 2016 + RPwD Rules. Disability certificate / UDID card must be issued within the time limit in the Rules (the Chief Commissioner for PwD has cited three months and flagged delays in Kerala). Complaints about denial or delay go to the State Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Kerala. Office code: DIS.
5. NATIONAL TRUST ACT, 1999 (autism, cerebral palsy, intellectual disability incl. Down syndrome, multiple disabilities). Sec 14: legal guardian appointed by the Local Level Committee (LLC) chaired by the District Collector; parents can nominate a future guardian. Niramaya health insurance for these conditions. Office code: GRD.
6. FAMILY PENSION FOR DISABLED CHILDREN (Kerala Service Rules, G.O.(P) 59/2024/Fin): children of government pensioners with permanent disability can receive family pension for life; a permanent UDID card is accepted instead of a separate medical certificate.
7. KERALA STATE SENIOR CITIZENS COMMISSION (Kerala State Senior Citizens Commission Act, 2025): statutory body for complaints about neglect, mistreatment and rights of senior citizens. Kerala Department of Senior Citizens formed May 2026.
8. ESCALATION: CM's Public Grievance Redressal Cell (cmo.kerala.gov.in); Vayomithram units (Kerala Social Security Mission) for elderly support; Ashwasakiran caregiver allowance for those caring for bedridden / severely disabled persons.
